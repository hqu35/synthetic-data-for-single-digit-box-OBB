# Borderless register crop

Open [the rendered sample](images/000001.png). The worker hides the outer
aperture lips and glass seat, keeps the continuous refractive cover, and
crops the sheared image to the five numeral-band boxes. The glass shader
concentrates seeded deposits in one soft-edged patch. Global sensor blur
and existing glass scratches remain active. Labels use final crop coordinates.

The preview uses seed 2026110711 and stain strength 0.85.
